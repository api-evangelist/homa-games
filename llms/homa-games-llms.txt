# Homa Games

> Homa is a global mobile game developer and publisher (Paris, France) whose Homa Lab platform pairs data-driven tooling with creative expertise to help studios ship commercially successful hyper-casual and hybrid-casual games. Its developer surface centers on the Homa SDK (Homa Belly), a no-code Unity meta-SDK. Homa exposes an SDK and developer platform rather than a public REST API.

## Developer Platform
- [Homa Lab](https://www.homagames.com/homa-lab): Data-driven game development platform (Ideas, Market Watcher, Homa SDK, Data Analytics, Submissions & Creatives, Homa Academy).
- [Homa SDK (Homa Belly)](https://www.homagames.com/homa-lab/homa-sdk): No-code Unity meta-SDK for consent, analytics, mediation, attribution and A/B ("N-testing").
- [Homa Lab Login](https://lab-v2.homagames.com/login)
- [Homa Lab Registration](https://lab-v2.homagames.com/registration)
